About Us

Built on Faith. Driven by Purpose. United as One.

At Texas Creed, we believe talent may win games, but character builds champions.

Texas Creed is a competitive youth baseball organization based in the Houston, Texas area, founded on the belief that developing great baseball players begins with building strong character.

Our mission extends far beyond wins, losses, and championships. We are committed to developing young athletes through discipline, accountability, teamwork, hard work, and respect for the game.

Our coaching philosophy emphasizes fundamentals, continuous development, and healthy competition. We challenge every player to improve, embrace adversity, support their teammates, and understand that success is earned through preparation and effort.

Rooted in Christian values, Texas Creed strives to lead by example, teaching our players the importance of faith, humility, integrity, and serving others. We believe the lessons learned between the lines should carry into the classroom, the community, and everyday life.

We are more than a baseball team. We are a family of players, coaches, and parents united by a common purpose: to raise young men who compete with confidence, lead with character, and represent something greater than themselves.
