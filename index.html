    How card fees are estimated

    So the budget never comes up short at checkout.

    3.5% on every card paymentPerfect Game adds a 3.5% tech fee to credit and debit cart payments. Fall checkouts matched it exactly ($13.13 on $375, $15.75 on $450).
    Built into each totalA $625 entry budgets $646.88 and a $475 entry budgets $491.63. The fee is rounded to the cent, per event.
    Bank payment saves itPaying by bank account, where Perfect Game allows it, skips the fee. Anything saved stays in the spring fund.
